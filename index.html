Kip

A running-analysis service by text message, from Max Sauers LLC. Kip reads the runs from your watch, tells you what each workout was and how it compared to your own past runs, answers your questions about your running, and plans your weekly miles around your calendar, the weather and your own rules.

How to sign up

Kip is invite-only for now. Once you have been invited, text (872) 273-7477 from your phone. By texting this number, you agree to receive SMS messages from Kip (Max Sauers LLC): a short analysis after each run you record, a weekly planning message, an occasional weather heads-up, account messages such as a link to connect your watch, and replies to your questions. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can stop at any time by replying STOP, and get help by replying HELP.

Kip's replies are written by AI. Before it reads your running data, Kip asks for your OK by text; see the Privacy Policy.

What the messages look like

You: What was my last run?

Kip: Your 3 x 3 mi is analyzed. Reps averaged 5:33/mi, 11 to 16 sec/mi faster than Aug 26 at about the same heart rate. Reply to ask more. Reply STOP to stop, HELP for help.

You: How many miles do I have left this week?

Kip: 45 mi left over 3 more days, target still 55.

Message details

Message frequency varies: typically one message after each run you record, one weekly planning message, an occasional weather heads-up, and replies to anything you ask. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.
